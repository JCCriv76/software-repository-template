# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants must communicate professionally, assume good intent while examining evidence, and
make the project welcoming regardless of identity, background, experience, employment status, or
role. Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and deliberate
disruption are not acceptable.

Technical disagreement is expected. Critique the work and its consequences, not the person.
Reviewers should explain blocking concerns clearly; authors should receive a timely path to resolve
them.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, meetings, chat, and other spaces
where someone represents the project or organization.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to repository administrators or the organization's designated
people team. Do not ask an AI assistant to mediate or retain sensitive personnel matters.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, pause discussions, restrict participation, or escalate to
organization leadership. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical and retaliation is
prohibited.
